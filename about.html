ABOUT THE BUSINESS

A polished website path for small businesses that do not want the usual overwhelm.

Who Web Dev Lady helps

Small brands that want to look established online.

Web Dev Lady is built for small business owners, service providers, creators, and growing brands that need a polished online presence without getting buried in jargon, agency layers, or a huge custom process too early.

Why the business exists

To make professional websites feel more accessible.

The goal is simple: give businesses a faster, clearer, more affordable way to launch. That means professional design, cleaner structure, and stronger brand presentation without forcing every client into a high-ticket build from day one.

How the work is offered

Flexible options that grow with the business.

Some clients need a ready-made template. Others need customization, setup support, or a fully custom website. Web Dev Lady offers each path so clients can start where they are and upgrade only when they truly need more support.
