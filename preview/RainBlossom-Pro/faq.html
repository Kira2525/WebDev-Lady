Tours & Booking

Start with the basics.

These questions help guests understand how tours, availability, booking, and event planning usually work at Rain Blossom.

Do I need an appointment to tour the estate?

Yes. Private estate tours are available by appointment so the team can give you a focused walkthrough of the ceremony spaces, reception areas, suites, and gardens.

How far in advance should I book?

Popular wedding dates can reserve months in advance. Reaching out early gives you more flexibility with dates, spaces, and package options.

What information should I send before requesting details?

Your preferred date or season, estimated guest count, event type, and any must-have spaces are helpful starting points.

Can I request package pricing before touring?

Yes. You can request package information first, then schedule a private tour once you have a better idea of the best fit.

Venue Details

Spaces, guests, and event flow.

Rain Blossom is designed for smooth movement between ceremony spaces, portrait areas, lounges, receptions, and guest gathering points.

How many guests can Rain Blossom host?

The estate can host celebrations up to 250 guests, depending on selected spaces, layout, event style, and package details.

Can we host both ceremony and reception onsite?

Yes. Full estate packages can include ceremony spaces, reception areas, preparation suites, and guest-friendly gathering areas.

Are private events allowed?

Yes. Rain Blossom can be used for bridal showers, anniversary dinners, private dinners, retreats, editorial shoots, and brand gatherings.

Are preparation suites included?

Selected packages may include private preparation suites. Availability depends on the package, schedule, and spaces reserved.

Vendors & Weather

Practical details matter.

The goal is a celebration that feels polished without guests noticing the logistics. These details help keep the day comfortable.

Do you allow outside vendors?

Yes. Outside vendors are welcome. Preferred vendor recommendations may also be available if you want help finding trusted options.

What happens if it rains?

Rain Blossom offers covered and indoor options so your celebration can continue comfortably while still keeping the garden atmosphere intact.

Can vendors tour the space before the event?

Vendor walkthroughs can usually be arranged after booking so teams can confirm layouts, timing, delivery access, and setup needs.

Can we take photos around the estate?

Yes. The gardens, pathways, lounges, and reception areas are designed with photography in mind.
