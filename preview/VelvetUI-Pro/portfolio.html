Before and After Direction

The portfolio is less about a single style and more about the shift from disconnected rooms to rooms that feel resolved.

Before

Spaces often arrive with good pieces but no shared rhythm.

  • Traffic flow feels cramped or unclear
  • Materials read colder or flatter than intended
  • Lighting and styling layers stop before the room feels complete
  • Guest-facing spaces photograph weaker than they feel in person

After

The finished room feels warmer, calmer, and easier to trust.

  • Layout decisions support everyday use and hosting
  • Textures and finishes build a more grounded atmosphere
  • Rooms carry one coherent story instead of competing ideas
  • The final styling pass makes the space feel publishable and lived in
