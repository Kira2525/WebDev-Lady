Pricing FAQ

Reusable pricing questions buyers can customize later.

Are these final pricing values?

No. These are demo tier values designed to help buyers preview a polished pricing page and replace the numbers later.

Can the pricing page work for one-time offers?

Yes. Swap the monthly framing for project, package, annual, or one-time language depending on the offer.

Can I add more tiers or a custom plan?

Yes. The card structure is reusable, so extra tiers or an enterprise contact option can be added without changing the whole system.
