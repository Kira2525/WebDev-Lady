# FreshNest Cleaning Co. Image Guide

This guide explains where images are used throughout the template and the recommended image sizes for the best quality and performance.

---

# Image Folder

Store all images inside the **assets** folder.

```text id="znzxgv"
assets/

favicon.png
favicon-192.png
favicon-512.png

freshnest-hero.png

gallery-1.jpg
gallery-2.jpg
gallery-3.jpg
gallery-4.jpg

logo.png (optional)
```

---

# Hero Image

**File Name**

```text id="jts0vd"
freshnest-hero.png
```

**Recommended Size**

```text id="4p7gcu"
1920 × 1080 pixels
```

**Recommended Format**

* PNG
* JPG
* WebP

**Tips**

* Use a high-quality cleaning or housekeeping photo.
* Leave open space on the left side for the heading and buttons.
* Avoid placing text inside the image.
* Keep the image bright and professional.

# Image Optimization

For faster loading:

* Resize large photos before uploading.
* Compress images when possible.
* Use JPG for photographs.
* Use PNG for logos and transparent images.
* Use WebP for the best balance of quality and file size (if supported).

---

# Replacing Images

1. Open the **assets** folder.
2. Replace the existing image with your own.
3. Keep the same filename to avoid updating the HTML or CSS.

OR

Rename your image and update the filename in:

* `index.html`
* `css/styles.css`

# Tips for Best Results

* Use bright, high-resolution photos.
* Keep a consistent editing style across all images.
* Avoid blurry or pixelated images.
* Optimize images to improve page load speed.
* Make sure images are relevant to your business and services.
