How Visits Work

Simple planning, clear arrival expectations, and a guided flow once you are on site.

1. Choose your format

Select a public night, school session, private event, or add-on like astrophotography support.

2. Confirm your details

Use the booking form to share guest count, preferred date, and any special accessibility or pacing needs.

3. Enjoy the experience

Our team handles orientation, live hosting, and telescope guidance so your group can stay focused on the sky.

4. Follow up with confidence

Schools and private groups receive clear coordination notes before the event and next-step guidance if weather changes.
