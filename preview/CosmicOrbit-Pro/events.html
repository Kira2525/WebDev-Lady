Events

Plan around the sky's most exciting nights with events made for shared discovery.

Cosmic Orbit events combine telescope access, live hosting, and themed programming so guests can experience special celestial moments with structure and excitement.

Meteor Shower Watch Parties

Peak-night gatherings with host commentary, sky cues, and observation-lawn seating.

Best for guests who want a relaxed but guided atmosphere during major meteor shower activity.

Moon Viewing Nights

Lunar-focused evenings with telescope stations aimed at the moon's most visible features.

Especially popular with first-time guests, families, and school groups who want an easy entry point.

Family Space Nights

Interactive events that blend storytelling, sky games, and shorter telescope rotations for younger audiences.

Great for weekends, school breaks, and multigenerational groups.

Featured Workshops

Hands-on astronomy sessions for guests who want more context, technique, or deeper conversation.

Astronomy Workshop

Reading the sky without guesswork.

Learn how to locate major constellations, track planets, and understand what changes from season to season.

Beginner Astrophotography

Simple techniques for capturing a night worth remembering.

Guests learn how to stabilize shots, frame lunar subjects, and get better results with accessible gear.

Space Storytelling

Planet, mission, and deep-sky talks that make the science feel alive.

A strong fit for museum nights, educational partnerships, and audiences that want a guided presentation before viewing.
