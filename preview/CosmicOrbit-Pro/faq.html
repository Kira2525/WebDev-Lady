Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for first-time visitors, school planners, and private group organizers.

These FAQs cover the topics guests ask most often before booking a night with Cosmic Orbit. They are also easy for customers to customize for their own live operations.

How does booking work?

Guests submit the booking form with their preferred date, guest count, and experience type. For a live site, that form can be connected to Formspree, Netlify Forms, EmailJS, or a custom backend.

What happens if weather affects visibility?

Outdoor sessions are monitored in advance. Depending on the business setup, guests can be offered a reschedule, alternate date, or indoor presentation format.

Are there age limits for family events?

Most family-friendly programs are designed for school-age children and up, but age guidance can vary by event length, time slot, and pacing.

Do guests need to bring their own equipment?

No personal telescope is required for most public nights. Guests typically just need weather-appropriate layers, water, and optional cameras or binoculars.

What is the refund or reschedule policy?

This demo template suggests a clearly written reschedule and refund policy on the live site. Private groups may also want deposits and date-change timelines.

Can Cosmic Orbit support accessibility needs?

Yes. The booking and contact forms include space for mobility, seating, sensory, and pacing requests so staff can plan ahead with care.
