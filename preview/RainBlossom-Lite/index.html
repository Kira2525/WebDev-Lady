Questions

Need the details before booking?

Here are common questions about tours, vendors, weather plans, guest count, packages, and event timing.

How do we schedule a private tour?

Use the contact form below to request a tour date. A team member will follow up with available times and next steps.

What is the guest capacity?

Rain Blossom can host up to 250 guests depending on the event setup, package, and selected venue spaces.

Do you allow outside vendors?

Yes. Outside vendors are welcome, but they must follow venue setup, safety, and cleanup guidelines.

Is there a weather backup plan?

Yes. Indoor and covered spaces are available for weather adjustments based on the package and event layout.

Can we host private events besides weddings?

Yes. Rain Blossom can be used for showers, dinners, retreats, anniversaries, brand events, and private celebrations.
