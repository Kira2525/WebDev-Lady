FAQ

Everything you need to know before you step aboard.

If you are choosing between a snorkel, a dive, or a private charter, these are the questions our reservations team answers most often.

Do I need to be certified to join a scuba trip?

Yes for our standard dive departures. Certified divers can join reef and wreck outings, while first-time ocean guests often choose our Coral Garden Snorkel or request a private introduction experience.

What is included with snorkeling and diving tours?

Shared excursions include captain and guide support, safety briefings, bottled water, and core equipment. Dive trips include tanks and weights, while snorkel outings include mask, fins, and flotation gear.

What happens if weather or sea conditions change?

If conditions are not safe or enjoyable for your planned route, we will recommend a better departure window, move to a calmer site, or help you reschedule based on availability.

Can you arrange private charters for celebrations or family groups?

Absolutely. Our private charters are popular for birthdays, proposals, family travel, and photographer-led wildlife days. Send us your preferred date, group size, and wish list through the inquiry form below.
