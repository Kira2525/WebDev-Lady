FAQ Preview

Answer the most common questions on the same page.

Who is the Lite version best for?

It is best for smaller businesses or sellers who want a lower-priced one-page offer with premium styling and simpler setup.

What is removed compared to Pro?

Lite removes the multi-page architecture, advanced content depth, and full Pro page inventory while keeping the core visual identity.

Can buyers still customize the content easily?

Yes. The one-page structure is straightforward to edit and the included docs explain where to update copy, images, and brand details.

Can this be used for other industries?

Yes. Buyers can adapt the design for travel, resorts, conservation, aquariums, tours, or any brand that benefits from a cinematic ocean theme.
