Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for clients planning their next move.

These are the questions teams most often ask about timelines, deliverables, consultations, and how our location and market insight engagements work.

Reports Briefings are designed to be readable, premium, and immediately useful in internal discussions.
Timelines Most focused engagements move quickly once the business question and scope are defined.
Consultations Strategy calls help translate the findings into practical next steps for your team.
What does a typical WorldView Global engagement include?
Most engagements include a discovery step, a premium written briefing or report, and a follow-up consultation to review findings and next-step recommendations.
How long does a project usually take?
Focused briefings can often be delivered within one to two weeks depending on complexity, while larger custom scopes may require a longer delivery window.
Do you work with specific industries only?
No. We regularly support travel, SaaS, education, agencies, conferences, and mission-led organizations, but the frameworks are flexible enough for many premium growth and strategy questions.
What kind of questions are best suited for a strategy call?
Strategy calls are ideal when you are comparing launch regions, reviewing an existing report, prioritizing audience opportunities, or pressure-testing a location decision with leadership in mind.
Can you tailor the report around one region or market?
Yes. Many clients ask for a narrowly scoped engagement focused on one geography, corridor, city cluster, or audience segment rather than a broad global view.
Do you provide raw data files?
Our primary deliverables are decision-ready summaries, frameworks, and advisory recommendations. If a project requires supporting materials or source notes, we can discuss that during scoping.
Can you support leadership presentations or internal meetings?
Yes. We can structure engagements so the briefing is presentation-friendly and can participate in a follow-up session to answer questions with your internal team.
How do we get started?
Start with a consultation request through the contact page. We will review your question, suggest the best-fit package or custom scope, and outline timing.
