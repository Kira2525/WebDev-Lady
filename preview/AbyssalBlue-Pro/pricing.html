Before You Book

Common pricing questions.

Are prices per person?

Beginner and advanced dive prices are listed per guest. Private charter pricing is listed as a starting rate and may vary by group size, route, schedule, and add-on services.

Is equipment included in every package?

Basic equipment is included in the beginner package. Advanced and private experiences include premium equipment options, with additional upgrades available on request.

Can guests reschedule?

Rescheduling may be available for weather, ocean conditions, or approved schedule changes. Contact the team as early as possible if your plans change.
