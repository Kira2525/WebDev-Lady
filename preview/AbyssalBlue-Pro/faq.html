Do visitors need diving experience?

No. Beginner experiences are designed for first-time divers with trained guides, safety briefings, and shallow-water routes. Advanced packages are available for certified or experienced divers.

Is equipment included?

Most guided experiences include essential equipment such as masks, fins, tanks, and safety gear. Specific equipment details may vary by package, so guests should review their booking confirmation before arrival.

What should guests bring?

Guests should bring swimwear, towels, reef-safe sunscreen, water, a valid ID, and any required certification documents for advanced dives.

Can children participate?

Some experiences are family-friendly, while others may have age, height, swimming ability, or certification requirements. Contact the team before booking if you are planning a visit with children.

What happens if weather is bad?

Guest safety comes first. If weather or ocean conditions are unsafe, the experience may be rescheduled, credited, or moved to another available time based on the reservation policy.

How early should guests arrive?

Guests should arrive 30 to 45 minutes before the scheduled experience for check-in, equipment fitting, safety instructions, and final weather updates.

Are private charters available?

Yes. Private charters are available for couples, families, small groups, school trips, creator shoots, resort excursions, and custom ocean experiences.

Can we book a group visit?

Yes. Group visits can be arranged for schools, families, travel groups, aquariums, resorts, and private events. Contact the team with your group size, preferred date, and experience type.

Are wildlife encounters guaranteed?

Marine wildlife is unpredictable. Guides choose routes based on safety, conditions, and recent activity, but specific animal sightings cannot be guaranteed.

How do I change or cancel a reservation?

Contact the team as soon as possible with your reservation details. Changes and cancellations depend on availability, notice period, weather, and the policy listed in your confirmation.
