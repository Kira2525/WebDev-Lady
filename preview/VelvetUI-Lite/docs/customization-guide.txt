VelvetUI Lite Customization Guide

Text:
- Update headlines, paragraphs, FAQ items, and button text in index.html.

Colors:
- Change the root variables at the top of css/style.css.

Layout:
- Reorder or remove sections directly in index.html.
- Keep section IDs matched to navigation anchors.

Animation:
- Edit the words array in js/script.js.
- Keep the canvas element in index.html if you want the signature VelvetUI effect.

Lite vs Pro:
- Lite is a one-page starter setup.
- Pro includes the full multi-page website system.
