Quick Notes

Most projects begin with a short discovery conversation and a right-sized recommendation.

  • Projects are scoped around room count, goals, and sourcing complexity
  • Design, furnishing, and renovation support can be combined into one proposal
  • High-touch collaboration is part of every engagement
What kinds of projects are the best fit for VELVET STUDIO?

Most engagements are a strong fit for homeowners, boutique rental hosts, and small hospitality teams that want warmer spaces, clearer room flow, and more collected design decisions.

Do you work room by room or only on full homes?

Both. One-room refreshes are a core offer, but the studio also handles multi-room projects, furnishing plans, and larger whole-home scopes when the space needs a broader point of view.

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects land between five and twelve weeks. Single-room design plans can move faster, while renovation overlap or extended sourcing can add time.

How do you price projects?

Projects are priced by scope, room count, sourcing complexity, and the level of install support required. The investment page shows starting ranges, and a tailored proposal follows the initial inquiry.

Can you work with furniture or decor we already own?

Yes. That is common. The studio often blends existing pieces with new sourcing so the home feels more personal and less newly purchased all at once.

Do you help during renovations?

Absolutely. VELVET STUDIO can guide finish palettes, fixture choices, lighting direction, and adjacent room cohesion so the renovation feels more resolved before selections are locked in.

What should we prepare before inquiring?

It helps to know the rooms involved, your rough timing, and whether the main need is layout clarity, sourcing help, styling, or renovation support. Even a short overview is enough to begin.

What happens after we submit the inquiry form?

You receive a confirmation first, then a follow-up recommendation based on the scope. From there, the studio can suggest the best package, timing, and next steps.
