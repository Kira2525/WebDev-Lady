FAQ Preview

Clear starter answers for buyers who want simplicity without losing quality.

Who is VelvetUI Lite designed for?

It works well for creators, personal brands, portfolios, simple offers, and small launches that only need one premium page.

Can I customize the text and colors easily?

Yes. The files are plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, so text, color variables, buttons, and sections are simple to update.

Does Lite include the same animated diamond system as Pro?

Yes. Lite keeps the same signature diamond text background and the same customizable words array.

When should I upgrade to Pro?

Upgrade when you want a full multi-page system with deeper content sections, more selling space, and more page-specific flexibility.
