COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers before you buy.

What is the difference between Lite and Pro templates?

Lite templates are single-page websites priced at $59. Pro templates are multi-page websites priced at $99 and include more sections, pages, and stronger layouts.

How much do templates cost?

Every Lite template is $59 and every Pro template is $99. Pricing is the same across CosmicOrbit, Abyssal Blue, VelvetUI, and WorldView.

Are the templates responsive and editable?

Yes. All templates include responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files that you can edit for your own business or brand.

Do templates include a commercial use license?

Yes. Each template includes a commercial use license plus documentation to help you work with the files.

What is template customization?

Template customization is a separate service that starts at $199. It covers logo placement, color changes, text replacement, image swaps, button and link updates, contact info updates, and basic section cleanup.

What is Website Setup Service?

Website Setup Service starts at $499 and is designed for clients who want a more complete done-with-you launch. It includes template installation help, logo and brand updates, content replacement, social links, contact form setup, domain connection assistance, mobile review, and a final website review.

How do I choose between customization and setup service?

Choose Template Customization if you mainly need branded visual and content edits. Choose Website Setup Service if you also want launch help, connection guidance, and a more complete review before going live.

Do I have to buy the template before customization?

Yes. Template customization is separate from the template purchase unless it is included in a custom quote for a larger project.

What do you need from me for customization?

Please send your business name, template name, preferred colors, logo, images, text, links, social media details, pages needed, and deadline if you have one.

Can you build something fully custom instead?

Yes. Custom website builds start at $999, with Business Website projects from $1,499, E-Commerce Website projects from $1,999, and Premium Custom Website projects from $2,999.
