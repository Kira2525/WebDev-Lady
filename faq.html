BEFORE YOU BUY

Common questions from buyers.

What is the difference between Lite and Pro templates?

Lite templates are streamlined single-page website designs. Pro templates include additional pages, expanded layouts, and more advanced sections for a fuller launch.

Are the templates responsive?

Yes. Web Dev Lady templates are built to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts using responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Do I get editable files?

Yes. Your download includes editable code files so you can update text, images, colors, links, and sections for your business or brand.

Can I use the templates commercially?

Yes. Each template includes a commercial use license so you can use it for your own business, service, brand, or digital product website.

How do downloads work after checkout?

After successful payment, you are sent to the matching thank-you page for your Lite or Pro purchase where your template download is available.

Can you customize a template for me?

Yes. If you want your logo, brand colors, copy, images, or business details added for you, contact Web Dev Lady for template customization services.
